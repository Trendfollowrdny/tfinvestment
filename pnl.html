Futures Account Profit and Loss Details

Investor Roster

Each investor is managed as a separate book — own capital, own FX on each contribution, own P&L. Click a row to open that investor.

① Input Panel — Periods

Add one block per period (e.g. Jul 9–Jul 31, Aug 1–Aug 31). Each period keeps its own seed, profit, loss, trades and win rate. All amounts are in USD.

② Input Panel — Capital & FX

One row per KRW→USDT conversion. The rate you actually got on that date, not a market average. A contribution dated inside a period is treated as capital added during that period, not as profit.
